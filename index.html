Privacy Policy for Call & Meet

Formerly "New Best". Last updated: October 22, 2026

Call & Meet ("we", "us", "our") is a dating app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Who can use the app

The app is for adults aged 18 and over. It is available in Uganda, Kenya, Tanzania, Ghana, Nigeria, South Africa, Egypt and Morocco. You only see and are seen by people in the same country as your phone number.

2. Information we collect

  • Account: your phone number (this is your login), a password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form), name, date of birth, gender and at least one profile photo.
  • Recovery email: an email address you add, either by choosing a Google account or by typing it in. It is required outside Uganda and optional in Uganda. We use it only to send you password reset and account recovery messages. It is never shown on your profile.
  • Profile: interests, what you are looking for, bio, status and any extra photos you add.
  • Messages and calls: the messages, voice notes and photos you send in chats, and records of calls (who called whom, when and for how long). We do not record the audio or video of calls.
  • Location: your approximate location, if you allow it, to show distance and nearby people.
  • Country signals: the country of your phone number, plus the country reported by your SIM card and mobile network. If neither is available, we may use the approximate country of your internet connection (IP address). These help us show you the right country and spot misuse.
  • Payments: in Uganda, details of mobile money payments (the phone number used, amount, and whether it succeeded). In other countries, subscriptions are paid through Google Play: we receive the subscription type, its status and its renewal date from Google. We never receive or store your card details.
  • Device and usage: device type, operating system, app version, a notification token, crash reports, and how you use the app (for example screens opened and features used).

3. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account, and to let you reset your password.
  • To show your profile to other users and show you theirs, and to run chat, calls, likes and waves.
  • To process subscriptions and unlock Premium features.
  • To keep the app safe. Profile photos are checked by automated tools (including Google AI services) for things like underage users, fake or non-human photos, and phone numbers in photos. Profiles may be hidden while they are reviewed.
  • To send you notifications about messages, calls and activity.
  • To fix problems and improve the app.

4. How we share your information

  • With other users: your name, photos, age, interests, approximate distance and online status are shown to people in your country. In Ghost Mode your photo is hidden. In Uganda only, a user who pays to reveal your contact may see your phone number. Your recovery email is never shown to other users.
  • Service providers: we use Google Firebase and Google Cloud (accounts, database, file storage, notifications, analytics and crash reports), Google Play (payments outside Uganda), a mobile money payment provider (payments in Uganda), and Google AI services (photo checks). They process data only to provide these services to us.
  • Legal reasons: we may share information if the law requires it, or to protect users and the app.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Cloud and Firebase servers, which may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Data is encrypted in transit.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile, change or add your recovery email (My Account), switch to Ghost Mode, and turn location on or off.
  • You can cancel a Google Play subscription at any time in Google Play → Subscriptions.
  • You can delete your account from the app, or request deletion, access or correction by emailing us (see below). We complete requests within 30 days.

7. Data retention

Chat messages are deleted 24 hours after they are seen. Other data is kept while your account is active. When you delete your account, we remove your data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example payment records).

8. Children

The app is only for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we find such an account, we remove it. Contact us if you think a minor is using the app.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will tell you about important changes in the app. The date at the top shows the latest version.
